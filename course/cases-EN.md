# CONTEMPORARY HORECA SCENE — CASE DATABASE (EN)

One-page profiles for seminar work. Fields: format, city, why it matters, teaching angle.

## Rankings & competitions

**MICHELIN Guide.** Anonymous inspectors; stars (★ outstanding in its category / ★★ worth a detour / ★★★ worth a special journey), Bib Gourmand, Green Star (in transition to the "Mindful Voices" initiative in 2026), special awards (Service, Sommelier, Young Chef). 2026 reveals used in class: Tokyo (Myojaku → three stars; 18 new stars), California (Californios — first Mexican restaurant worldwide with three stars), Toronto (Restaurant Pearl Morissette two stars). History angle: MICHELIN Guide Moscow debuted in 2021 (first stars in Russia/CIS) and was suspended in 2022 — a governance & geopolitics case.

**The 50 Best (the50.com, formerly "50 Best").** Global academy of anonymous experts; restaurants' voters name their 8 best experiences; bars academy 600+ experts, 7 votes each. Restaurant ceremony 2026 — Lima, 4 Nov (Teatro Municipal); pre-announced 2026 Art of Hospitality Award: Tuju (São Paulo). Bars 2025 revealed in Hong Kong; 2026 extended list 51–100 already published (Sept 2026). Teaching angle: voting design, list economics, city-branding effects (~$2M projected spend in Lima).

**GreatList (greatlist.ru).** International guide founded 2022; 100+ experts; anonymous, self-paid, repeated visits; criteria: food & chef imagination; service across the full CJM; design & ergonomics; atmosphere from scent to music. Cities: Moscow, St. Petersburg, Yekaterinburg, Kazan, Nizhny Novgorod, Russian Far East; Dubai; Doha; Bangkok; Hong Kong; Shanghai; Singapore & Seoul soon. GreatList Sessions 2025 — guest dinners in Moscow by world chefs. Teaching angle: an alternative, city-expert rating model; how to audit atmosphere.

**Diageo World Class.** 16 seasons; 51 countries in 2025; finals in Toronto; winner Felice Capasso (Norway, Nedre Løkka Cocktailbar, Oslo; founder, Sesto Senso Academy). Challenges: Johnnie Walker Black Label classic reinventions ("Top Notes" after the French 75); Don Julio 1942 serve inspired by an AI artwork; Singleton multisensory serve with a custom "That's Amore" record sleeve. Jury: Eric Van Beek (Handshake Speakeasy), Monica Berg (Tayēr + Elementary), Ago Perrone (Connaught Bar). Mission "drink better, not more"; 450,000+ bartenders trained. Teaching angle: competitions as industry R&D and talent market; AI as creative brief.

## Restaurants

**Maido — Lima, №1 World's 50 Best Restaurants 2025.** Nikkei cuisine as cultural diplomacy; Lima holds four top-50 venues (Maido, Kjolle, Mérito, Mayta). Angle: city scene effects of a №1.

**Asador Etxebarri — Atxondo, №2.** Fire, product minimalism, one man and one grill. Angle: radical simplicity as luxury.

**Quintonil — Mexico City, №3.** Native Mexican ingredients in fine dining. Angle: regional identity going global.

**DiverXO — Madrid, №4 (Dabiz Muñoz).** Culinary punk, controlled chaos. Angle: chef's personality as brand.

**Alchemist — Copenhagen, №5.** Multi-hour holistic experience, 50 impressions. Angle: experience economy at the extreme.

**Gaggan — Bangkok, №6.** Progressive Indian, emoji menu legacy. Angle: reinvention after collapse.

**Sézanne — Tokyo, №7; Myojaku — Tokyo, three MICHELIN stars 2026.** Angle: Japanese precision, land-and-sea harmony; the deepest starred city scene.

**Californios — San Francisco, three MICHELIN stars 2026.** First Mexican restaurant worldwide with three stars. Angle: heritage cuisine entering the top tier.

**Tuju — São Paulo, Art of Hospitality 2026.** Purpose-built house; menus themed on rain, wind, drought; fewer guests, more time; reading glasses and drawing kits for kids. Angle: personalisation as hospitality philosophy.

**Gerbou — Dubai, Prix Versailles 2025 + MICHELIN recommendation.** Emirati gastronomy as an educational project. Angle: national cuisine as fine dining; architecture awards.

**Restaurant Pearl Morissette — Niagara (Toronto guide), two stars 2026.** Angle: terroir-driven tasting menus outside capitals.

## Bars

**Bar Leone — Hong Kong, №1 World's 50 Best Bars 2025.** "Cocktail popolari"; №1 two years after opening; first Asian №1. Angle: simplicity, warmth, hospitality-first design.

**Handshake Speakeasy — Mexico City, №2 (№1 in 2024).** Eric Van Beek; hidden-door dramaturgy, flawless execution. Angle: speakeasy as theatre.

**Sips / Paradiso — Barcelona, №3/№4.** Barcelona as cocktail capital; Sips = Best Bar in Europe, three years running. Angle: city cluster effects.

**Tayēr + Elementary — London, №5 (Monica Berg).** Innovation + sustainability leadership. Angle: R&D culture in a bar.

**Connaught Bar — London, №6 (Ago Perrone).** Hotel bar excellence; trolley ritual. Angle: heritage luxury, hotel F&B renaissance.

**Himkok — Oslo.** Best Bar Design (sustainable reimagining of a 200-year-old space); in-house distillery. Angle: design awards, circular production.

**The Cambridge Public House — Paris.** Sustainable Bar award, huge climber. Angle: sustainability as competitive strategy.

**Mimi Kakushi — Dubai (Best Bar in Middle East) / Hero Bar — Nairobi (Best Bar in Africa).** Angle: the widening map of cocktail culture.
